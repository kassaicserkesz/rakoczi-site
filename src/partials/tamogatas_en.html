•—• 2% of your tax

Assign 2% of your income tax – it costs you nothing

If you pay income tax in Slovakia, you can assign 2% of your paid tax (or up to 3% as a volunteer) directly to our troop. It is not an extra expense – you simply decide where part of your tax goes.

1

Request the certificate from your employer

If you are an employee, ask your employer for your annual tax reconciliation and to issue the Potvrdenie o zaplatení dane form.

2

Fill in the declaration

Download the Vyhlásenie form below – our troop's details are already pre-filled in it.

3

Submit it to the tax office

Submit both forms together to your local tax office by 30 April. If you file your own tax return, you can designate the troop directly in your return.

Exact steps and deadlines will be updated along with the final documents
