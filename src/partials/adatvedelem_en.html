For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at the e-mail address above.

2. What data do we process, why and for how long?

a) Contacting us (form or e-mail)

When you write to us, we process your name, e-mail address and the content of your message solely for the purpose of replying. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). We retain messages for up to one year after the inquiry is resolved.

b) Joining the troop

In the application form, we ask for details of the child and the parent (legal guardian): name, date of birth, contact details and information required for camps. Purpose: membership records and the safe organization of meetings and camps. Legal basis: consent and membership relationship (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR); any health-related data (e.g. allergies) is processed only with explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). Data is retained for the duration of membership and subsequently for the period required by law.

c) Photos and video recordings

Photos and videos may be taken at our events and published on our website and social media pages. Identifiable recordings of minors are published only with the consent of their legal guardian. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, and upon request we will promptly remove the recording. Simply send an e-mail to the address above.

d) Website operation and analytics

We measure website traffic using cookie-free, anonymous statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that do not collect personal data. For security and operational purposes, the hosting provider may keep technical logs (e.g. IP address) on the basis of legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising or profiling technologies. We store only a single setting in your browser's local storage (localStorage): your light/dark theme preference, which contains no personal data and can be deleted at any time in your browser settings.

4. Who has access to the data?

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing purposes. To operate the website, we use the following technical service providers (data processors):

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting and content delivery,
  • GitHub, Inc. — website content storage,
  • Web3Forms — forwarding messages from the contact form,
  • Google LLC — application form and calendar.

These providers ensure adequate protection of personal data under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. What are your rights?

Under the GDPR, you may at any time request:

  • access to the personal data we process about you,
  • rectification or erasure of your data,
  • restriction of processing, or object to processing,
  • your data in a portable format,
  • withdrawal of your previously given consent (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal).

You can submit your request to the e-mail address above. We will respond within 30 days at the latest. If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Slovak supervisory authority: Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, dataprotection.gov.sk.

6. Children's data

The activities of the scout troop primarily involve minors. We process minors' personal data exclusively with the consent of their legal guardian, strictly limited to what is necessary, and protect it with special care.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always available on this page.
