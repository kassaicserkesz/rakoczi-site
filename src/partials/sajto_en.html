•—• About us in short

Our troop in one paragraph

The 17th II. Rákóczi Ferenc Scout Troop has been operating in Košice since 1992 as a member organization of the Hungarian Scout Association in Slovakia (SZMCS). The troop organizes weekly patrol meetings, hikes and camps for Hungarian children and youth in Košice; most of its members are students or alumni of the Sándor Márai Hungarian-Language Secondary and Primary School. It is named after Francis II Rákóczi, whose final resting place is in the crypt of St. Elisabeth Cathedral in Košice. Its motto is: “Cum Deo pro Patria et Libertate!”
