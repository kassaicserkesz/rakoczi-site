Who we are

The 17th II. Rákóczi Ferenc Scout Troop has been active in Košice since 1992 as a member organization of the Hungarian Scout Association in Slovakia (SZMCS).

Our mission is to welcome Hungarian children and young people in Košice into a community where they can grow, make lifelong friends and experience what it means to do good together.

Most of our members are students or alumni of the Sándor Márai Hungarian-Language Secondary and Primary School. We hold regular weekly patrol meetings for ages 8–16, where children learn independence, responsibility and teamwork through play.

Our goal is to raise resourceful, responsible, reliable, diligent, nature-loving, accepting and respectful young people who live by Christian values and grow up to become good leaders, key members of their community and happy adults.

We recruit new members every two years from grades 2 and 3 of primary school, but older newcomers are always welcome as well.

•—• Our association

SZMCS

The Hungarian Scout Association in Slovakia (SZMCS) was re-established on 11 March 1990 and today has more than 1,400 members across Slovakia. Its goal is to educate Hungarian-speaking children and youth through the values of scouting: faith, community, love of nature and service.

SZMCS is a member of Slovak Scouting and the Forum of Hungarian Scout Associations, maintaining international ties with scouts around the world.

•—• Our namesake

The namesake of our troop

Our troop's namesake, Francis II Rákóczi (II. Rákóczi Ferenc), is one of the greatest figures in Hungarian history, leader of the war of independence and a symbol of national freedom.

Rákóczi's life and ideals – loyalty, courage, faith and patriotism – stand as an example for us in everyday scout life.

His connection to the city of Košice is especially strong. After passing away in exile in 1735, his remains were laid to final rest here in 1906 in the crypt of St. Elisabeth Cathedral. The city's streets, buildings and memorials, such as the Rodostó Memorial House, still preserve his name and spirit today.

For us, Francis II Rákóczi is not merely a historical figure, but a role model for perseverance, faith and service to the community – values that form the very foundation of scouting.

•—• Where we come from

History of our troop

1990 – 1992

Re-founding and investiture

The Hungarian Scout Association in Slovakia was officially re-established on 11 March 1990, and the scouts of Košice were invested on 22 May 1992 as troops 17 and 18: the 17th II. Rákóczi Ferenc Scout Troop and the 18th Kazinczy Ferenc Scout Troop. Two troops were formed because there were so many scouts at the time. In the very beginning, there was only one mixed patrol.

First recruitment

From church to school

This changed with the first recruitment drive. Announcements were made in churches, and since the scout den was next to the Premonstratensian monastery, interest was high. The other recruitment took place at the Sándor Márai School via the school loudspeaker. Several students who joined after that announcement remain active scouts and helpers at our events to this day.

6 May 1996

The two troops unite

The two Košice troops merged on 6 May 1996. Scoutmaster István Csajka became the new troop leader.

Today

One troop, shared memory

Today, one Hungarian scout troop operates in Košice: the 17th II. Rákóczi Ferenc Scout Troop. Our shared past is preserved in the troop's war cry:

„Egy hét-egy hét-egy tizenhét!
Egy nyolc-egy nyolc-egy tizennyolc!
Rákóczi! Kazinczy!
Ferenc-Ferenc-engem soha el nem feledsz!”

Today, scouts remain silent during the lines „Egy nyolc-egy nyolc-egy tizennyolc” (18) and „Kazinczy” in memory of the former sister troop.
